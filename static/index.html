AI INVESTIGATION AUDITOR (ACADEMIC / HACKATHON PROTOTYPE)
This application functions as a dedicated second-level investigation audit layer. It cross-references FIRs, witness testimonies, CCTV surveillance logs, evidence registries, timelines, and custody transfers to identify potential contradictions, timeline gaps, and missing document linkages. Strict Ethical Invariant: The system NEVER determines guilt or innocence and never accuses individuals. All findings are presented neutrally for authorized human investigator review.

🔍 Architectural Differentiation: Dedicated Audit Layer

❌ What This System Is NOT

  • NOT a crime-solving or predictive policing system
  • NOT a guilt/innocence determination engine
  • NOT a replacement for authorized human investigators
  • Does NOT invent or fabricate relationships without record backing

✅ What This System DOES

  • Cross-references multi-source investigation documents
  • Detects timestamp conflicts and unexplained timeline intervals
  • Identifies uncataloged evidence tokens and missing witness statements
  • Performs case-isolated RAG retrieval to present exact supporting excerpts
  • Provides structured human review workflow and formal audit reporting
