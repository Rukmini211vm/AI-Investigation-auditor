EXHIBITS RECEIVED FOR SCIENTIFIC EXAMINATION:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Exhibit E-001  : One (1) fired 9mm brass cartridge casing recovered from crime scene.
Exhibit E-003  : One (1) 9mm country-made semi-automatic pistol seized from Suspect S-001.
Exhibit E-004  : Three (3) live 9mm rounds found in the magazine.

LABORATORY FINDINGS & MICROSCOPIC COMPARISON:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Caliber Verification:
   Exhibit E-001 casing is confirmed 9x19mm Parabellum, headstamp 'KF-2022'.

2. Firing Pin Impression:
   The firing pin impression on Exhibit E-001 was subjected to comparison microscopy 
   against test-fired casings from seized pistol Exhibit E-003. Distinctive circular 
   striations and eccentric hemispherical indentations matched at 32x magnification.

3. Chamber & Breechface Marks:
   Parallel breechface shear marks on E-001 exhibit identical signature marks 
   consistent with the breech block of Exhibit E-003.

4. Chain of Custody & Preservation:
   Exhibits were received sealed with lac seal bearing impression 'INSP-POLICE-04'.
   Seals were intact upon receipt on 2024-10-13 at 14:30 hrs.
   After examination, exhibits were resealed under SFSL Seal #8491.

EXPERT OPINION:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The fired cartridge casing marked Exhibit E-001 was conclusively discharged from the 
firearm marked Exhibit E-003 to the exclusion of all other weapons tested.
